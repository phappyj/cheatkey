Privacy Policy

Short version: no account, no sign-up, no data sold. We use Google Analytics 4 for aggregate visit and click statistics, and the links that earn a commission belong to third parties whose own policies apply once you click them.

01Who runs this site

This site is a personal link hub operated by an individual publisher in Seoul, Republic of Korea, publishing under the name Work Cheatkey (YouTube @workcheatkeyai, Korean edition @cheatkey.work). It is not a company service and has no user accounts, logins, or payment functions.

Contact for any privacy question or request: phappyj@gmail.com.

02What we collect

We do not ask you for personal information, and there is no form on this site. We do not operate our own database of visitors.

Server logs (host)

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., part of Microsoft). As with any web server, GitHub receives standard technical data when your browser loads a page — IP address, browser and device type, requested URL, and timestamps — for security and abuse prevention. This is handled by GitHub under the GitHub Privacy Statement, not by us.

The copy buttons

The "Copy prompt" buttons use your browser's clipboard function locally on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to any server when you copy a prompt.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, which receives page views, clicks on outbound tool buttons, and technical data such as approximate location and device type. It does not receive anything you type. Details and opt-out are in section 03.

03Cookies and analytics

This site sets no advertising cookies and no tracking pixels, and does not store anything about you in your browser's local storage. Two kinds of measurement cookies can appear:

Google Analytics 4 (our own measurement)

We use Google Analytics 4 (property Work Cheatkey, measurement ID G-E23M7R0R8N, provided by Google LLC) to see at an aggregate level which pages and which buttons people use. It sets first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_<container-id>, which stay on your browser for up to two years. Through them Google receives: the page you viewed, the outbound tool button you clicked, a randomly generated client ID, and technical data your browser sends with any request — device type, browser, language, approximate location from your IP address, and referral source. We see only aggregate reports (page views, click counts by tool). We do not send Google your name, email address, or any value you type into this site, and we have not enabled Google Signals, advertising personalisation, or data sharing with Google's advertising products for this property.

Affiliate cookies (set by the partner, not us)

When you click an affiliate link, the destination site (for example Make.com or Decktopus) may set a cookie that records the visit came from this page so a commission can be attributed. Those cookies are set and governed by that third party.

You can refuse or delete all of these cookies in your browser settings; the site works the same without them. To opt out of Google Analytics across every site, install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

05Analytics provider and data retention

Analytics are provided by Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC, USA), under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Event data is kept for the retention period set in the property (14 months) and is used only for aggregate traffic and click statistics, never for advertising profiles.

The site also records two custom events with the tool name and the place on the page where the button sits: affiliate_click for outbound tool buttons, and filter_tools / prompt_copy for the category filter and the copy buttons. These carry no personal identifiers of their own.

06Your rights and retention

Because we hold no account data about visitors, there is nothing for us to export or correct on request. The analytics described in section 03 can be refused at any time through your browser settings or Google's opt-out add-on. For data held by the host or by a partner site, use the links above to exercise your rights with them directly — GitHub and Make.com both honour GDPR and similar data-subject requests. If you believe this site has handled something incorrectly, write to phappyj@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

The site is intended for a general professional audience and is not directed at children under 16.
