Privacy Policy

This page explains what happens to your information when you visit phappyj.github.io/cheatkey, including the English hub at /en/. Short version: no account, no sign-up, no data sold. The links that earn a commission belong to third parties, and their own policies apply once you click them.

01Who runs this site

This site is a personal link hub operated by an individual publisher in Seoul, Republic of Korea, publishing under the name Work Cheatkey (YouTube @workcheatkeyai, Korean edition @cheatkey.work). It is not a company service and has no user accounts, logins, or payment functions.

Contact for any privacy question or request: phappyj@gmail.com.

02What we collect

We do not ask you for personal information, and there is no form on this site. We do not operate our own database of visitors.

Server logs (host)

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., part of Microsoft). As with any web server, GitHub receives standard technical data when your browser loads a page — IP address, browser and device type, requested URL, and timestamps — for security and abuse prevention. This is handled by GitHub under the GitHub Privacy Statement, not by us.

The copy buttons

The "Copy prompt" buttons use your browser's clipboard function locally on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to any server when you copy a prompt.

03Cookies and local storage

This site does not set its own cookies, advertising pixels, or tracking identifiers, and does not store anything in your browser's local storage.

Cookies may appear after you leave this site. When you click an affiliate link, the destination site (for example Make.com) may set a cookie that records that the visit came from this page so that a commission can be attributed. Those cookies are set and governed by that third party, not by us.

05Analytics

This site currently loads no third-party analytics — no Google Analytics 4, no advertising tags. If Google Analytics 4 or a comparable tool is added later, this policy will be updated before it goes live, and the disclosure will name the provider and its cookie use. Any analytics we would use is configured for aggregate traffic statistics only, never for advertising profiles.

06Your rights and retention

Because we hold no personal information about visitors, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request. For data held by the host or by a partner site, use the links above to exercise your rights with them directly — GitHub and Make.com both honour GDPR and similar data-subject requests. If you believe this site has handled something incorrectly, write to phappyj@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

The site is intended for a general professional audience and is not directed at children under 16.
