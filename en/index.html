AI Prompts & Automation Workflows

2 prompts

Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini — anything. Fill the brackets, keep the rules. Both prompts work better when you give raw notes instead of tidy summaries.

AI · MEETING NOTES

Meeting notes 30 min → 30 sec

Three lines of setup and you never write minutes again. Works in any language — the output follows your notes.

  • 1Role"You are a senior PM. No fluff, no small talk."
  • 2MaterialPaste the notes as they are — don't summarise first.
  • 3FormatDecisions / Action items (owner + due) / Next agenda.
PromptYou are a senior project manager with 10 years of experience. Read the meeting notes below and build one table with only these three columns: 1) Decisions made 2) Action items — owner and due date 3) Topics to discuss next Rules: do not guess anything that is not in the notes — mark it "Needs confirmation". Keep each cell under 15 words. No intro, no summary paragraph. Meeting notes: [paste raw notes here]
Open ChatGPT ↗

Why it worksMost bad AI minutes come from a vague ask, not a weak model. "Decisions / owners / next" forces the model to throw away the 80% of a meeting that was just thinking out loud.

AI · EMAIL

Polite decline email 20 min → 20 sec

Say no without burning the relationship — and without rewriting it five times.

  • 1AudienceWho they are and how senior — one line.
  • 2Reason + alternativeWhy it's hard, and what you can do instead.
  • 3Tone lock"Polite but firm, max 3 sentences."
PromptYou are a 15-year office veteran writing an email for me. Use the four points below to write an email declining a request. 1) Recipient: [e.g. a team lead from another department, one level above me] 2) Request to decline: [e.g. they asked for a report by this Friday] 3) One-line reason it's hard: [e.g. my own deadline is already slipping this week] 4) What I can offer instead: [e.g. deliver it by Tuesday morning next week] Rules: polite but firm, three sentences maximum. Apologise once, never twice. No excuses, no emoji. End on the alternative. Also give me a one-line subject.
Open ChatGPT ↗

Scheduling? Put "two times that work for me" in slot 4 and it becomes a reschedule email instead.

Why it worksThe limit is the trick. Three sentences and one apology leaves no room for the rambling over-explanation that makes a "no" sound like a negotiation.
