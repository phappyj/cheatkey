Work Cheatkey

Short-form AI and productivity content for people who want to finish their work faster. This page is the one-pager for program managers, affiliate teams and brand partners. Last updated 19 November 2026.

01What we publish

Practical, screen-recorded tutorials on getting real work done with AI and automation tools: meeting notes in seconds, drafting difficult emails, cleaning up repetitive admin, comparing three tools and picking one. Every episode ends with a single next step the viewer can take, and the tools we recommend are tools we use ourselves.

Two channels, same editorial standard: an English channel aimed at US and global knowledge workers, and a Korean channel for Korean office workers. The Korean channel also runs a separate daily shopping-deal series for consumer tech; SaaS partnership content stays in the productivity episodes.

03Audience

Knowledge workers, office professionals and small teams who already pay for SaaS and are actively looking for ways to cut time on routine tasks — the buyer of a productivity tool, not a general-entertainment audience.

Straight answer on size: both channels launched in November 2026 and are early-stage. We are not going to quote inflated follower counts. Current scale is under 1,000 monthly visitors, with new episodes every day. Early partners get placement at the start of that curve.

04How we work with partners

  • Dedicated short — one episode built around the tool, hook, demo and a single call to action.
  • Integrated tutorial — the tool appears inside a workflow episode where it genuinely fits.
  • Comparison roundup — the tool placed in a 3-way comparison with a clear "pick one" conclusion.
  • Hub placement — a card or button in the relevant workflow guide on the web hub.

We mark affiliate links and sponsorships clearly in the video description and on the page, and we follow each program's brand and keyword rules.

05Rates and contact

Affiliate programs are the default. Paid placements are quoted per episode or per campaign depending on scope — tell us the goal and we will send a one-page plan with deliverables and dates.
