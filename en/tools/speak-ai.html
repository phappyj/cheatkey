01What we checked

How this page was made. Read on speakai.co/pricing on 25 Nov 2026 (plan cards, credit rates, trial terms). No Speak AI account was used for this page: transcription accuracy and language coverage are the vendor's claims, not our measurements.

05Questions people ask

Does Speak AI have a free plan?

No. There is a 7-day trial with no card required, then a paid plan or pay-as-you-go credits. A card is needed after the trial.

How many hours is 25 credits?

Speak AI equates 25 credits a seat to about 25 hours of standard transcription. Premium languages and meeting transcription cost more credits per hour.

Can it join my Zoom call?

Yes — Speak AI can join Zoom, Teams and Meet to record and transcribe, which is how the meeting-notes workflow on this site runs.
