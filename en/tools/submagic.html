01What we checked

  • Free: 15 credits a month. One captioned upload costs 3 credits, so about five videos; auto zooms, clean audio and other extras cost more on top.

05Questions people ask

How much is Submagic's free plan?

15 credits a month, enough for about five captioned uploads, no card required. Every export on the free plan carries a Submagic watermark.

Why did my credits go faster than five videos?

A captioned upload is 3 credits; auto zooms, clean audio and other extras each add credits, which leaves fewer videos.

Can I remove the watermark without paying?

No. Watermark removal is part of the paid plans, from $19/month or $12/month billed yearly.

Which plan do I need for longer clips?

Starter caps a video at 2 minutes; Pro allows 5 and Business 30, so pick by clip length before credit count.
