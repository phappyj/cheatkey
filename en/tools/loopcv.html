LoopCV's free plan is a monthly cap, not a trial — 10 applications and one job title

LoopCV takes your CV once, then applies for you every day: one “loop” per job title, per place. The free plan is real but small — one search, ten applications or emails and three job boards a month, no card and no time limit. Below is what each paid tier actually changes, and where the numbers on the sales page come from.

04Who it fits, and who it does not

Worth it if

One clearly named job title that you want applied to in volume, in a city the boards cover.

Skip it if

You rewrite every cover letter by hand — that is not what this automates.

The role is niche enough that the big boards do not carry it.

05Questions people ask

Is LoopCV free?

Yes, with a monthly cap: 1 job-title loop, up to 10 applications or emails, 3 job boards, no credit card and no time limit. Paid starts at €8.99/month.

Do I need a credit card for the free plan?

No. Their FAQ says the free plan needs no card, and the free plan does not expire.

Is it safe to let a tool apply for me?

It sends in your name, so proofread the email template once and watch the first week of applications before you widen it. On free you are limited to ten a month anyway.

Does it work outside the US?

A loop is a job title plus a place, so put your city or country in the loop. Which boards it can reach is capped by your plan.
