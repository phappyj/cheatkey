05Questions people ask

Is there a free plan?

No. Taja offers a 7-day free trial that optimises and repurposes up to 4 long-form videos. The trial needs no card.

What does the trial include?

4 long videos up to 2 hours each, 5 GB import, up to 5 shorts per video, titles, tags and thumbnails — but not watermark removal or downloads.

Does the trial let me download shorts?

No. The pricing compare table shows “–” for Download Shorts and watermark removal on the free-trial column, so exporting starts with a paid plan from $19/month.
