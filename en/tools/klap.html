01What we checked

How this page was made. One side-by-side run on 24 Nov 2026 with the same YouTube link and default settings, Klap's pricing page read on 24 Nov 2026, and Klap's own “Is Klap free?” FAQ. No clips were published from the test: exports need a paid plan.

  • Cuts land inside the clip: transcript gaps and waveform holes are removed, captions are burned in word by word, and the frame keeps following the speaker.
  • OpusClip on the same video: 18 clips, scores 84–73, but 4 re-cut the same Blender Cloud moment and 2 opened on a black frame. Its top pick (1:35) was one continuous take, and “Creative Commons” came back as “Graves Common”.

  • Source videos can run up to 3 hours, and you are billed per clip generated, not per minute.
