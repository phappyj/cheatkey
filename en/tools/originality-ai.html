05Questions people ask

Is Originality.ai free?

It has a one-time free allowance on the plan card (10,000 words / 100 credits). The compare table on the same page lists 60 credits with 3 scans a day, so read your balance in the app after signing up.

Why did 159 words cost 2 credits?

One credit covers 100 words and the charge rounds up per chunk, so a 159-word scan bills as two credits.

Can it detect Korean text?

Yes — our Korean samples were scanned and scored per sentence. A formal report register scored 97% AI, and the same content written as a memo scored 5% AI.

Should I trust the score?

As a review signal, yes: it points you at sentences worth rewriting. As proof that a person or a model wrote something, no — Originality's own guide says the score can be wrong.
